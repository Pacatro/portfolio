Hi, I’m Paco, and this is my brand new personal blog and portfolio.

## Why a blog?

Some parts of a project do not fit inside a repository description. This blog gives me a place to explain the decisions behind the code, document what I learn, and explore technical ideas in a little more depth.

You can expect notes about software engineering, artificial intelligence, low-level systems, and the process of building practical tools. Some posts will be technical, others will simply be short reflections from work in progress or curious things I learned.

## Start with the projects

If this is your first visit, the best place to begin is the [projects section](/#projects). It shows the breadth of my work and links directly to the source repositories.

Thanks for stopping by. This is only the first entry, and there is more to come.
